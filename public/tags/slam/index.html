EKF SLAM in ROS2

A ROS2 simulation of an omnidirectional robot that maps its surroundings while tracking its own position, using an extended Kalman filter, ICP scan matching, and an occupancy grid. Also includes an exploration of visual SLAM with ORB-SLAM3.
