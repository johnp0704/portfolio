Who Am I?

My name is John Poirier, and I’m an engineering student studying electrical engineering and robotics. I’m pursuing an M.S. in Robotics at Northeastern University, concentrating in Electrical Engineering, after graduating summa cum laude from the University of Vermont with a B.S. in Electrical Engineering and a Certificate in Autonomy and Robotics.

I enjoy working across a robot’s whole stack, from the PCBs and drivers to the controllers and perception that let it act on its own. As the software and autonomy lead on my senior capstone, I built the navigation, control, and machine learning for an autonomous weeding robot. Other projects range from a line-following robot on a PCB I designed to EKF SLAM in ROS2 and inverse kinematics for a robotic arm.

In the future, I hope to work in marine robotics, working on ROVs, floats, AUVs, UUVs, and other devices for ocean exploration and data acquisition.

I’m looking for Summer 2027 internships and Fall 2027 co-ops in robotics and autonomy. Take a look at my projects, or reach out through the Contact page.

Education & Experience

My education spans three universities in two countries: a B.S. in Electrical Engineering at the University of Vermont, including a semester abroad at the Auckland University of Technology in New Zealand, and now an M.S. in Robotics at Northeastern University.

Alongside my studies, I’ve designed power, lighting, and fire alarm systems for commercial buildings as an electrical engineering intern at DuBois & King, taught embedded systems as a teaching assistant for UVM’s Microcontroller Systems course, and researched GPU-accelerated optimization for renewable energy dispatch. Now I develop ROS 2 software for Northeastern’s MATE ROV competition team.
